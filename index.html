About Bright Tech Education

Bright Tech Education, led by Mayowa Samson Damilola, focuses on teaching ICT, Coding, Robotics, Web Development, and Graphic Design to primary school students. We provide practical, engaging, and easy-to-understand lessons that help students build strong digital skills for school and real-life applications.
